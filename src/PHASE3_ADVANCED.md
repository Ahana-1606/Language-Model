# Phase 3: Advanced Bengali TTS

Training a Bengali TTS model from scratch is advanced.

You usually need:

- hundreds of hours of Bengali audio
- high-quality transcripts
- a strong GPU
- knowledge of deep learning, audio preprocessing, and model training

For this project, prefer:

1. use `gTTS` for a working demo
2. collect clean Bengali audio/text data
3. fine-tune an existing multilingual TTS model

Only train from scratch after the simpler path works.
